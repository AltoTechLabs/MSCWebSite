Privacy Policy – My Soccer Center

Last updated: [add date]

My Soccer Center ("we", "our", or "the App") is a mobile application designed for players and team captains who participate in leagues managed by administrators using the My Soccer Center web platform.

1. Information We Collect

The My Soccer Center mobile app does not collect personal information directly from the user.

User accounts are created by league administrators through the My Soccer Center web platform. The app only displays the information already registered by the administrator.

2. Information Provided by League Administrators

User information is created and managed by league administrators. The app only reads and displays this data and does not collect or modify personal information.

3. Authentication

The App allows login with email/password or Google Sign-In. Google Sign-In only provides the authentication token needed to log in. No Google profile data is collected or stored.

4. Data Stored on the Device

The App stores only an authentication token to keep the user logged in. No other data is stored locally. The token is removed when the user logs out.

5. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to allow players to access their league information and to display tournament data such as schedules, results, and statistics.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share information with third parties. Data is only transmitted to our backend server to authenticate users and display league data. We do not use analytics or advertising services.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. All accounts must be created by administrators.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures such as HTTPS and token-based authentication to protect data.

9. Your Rights

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their information from their league administrator, who manages their account.
